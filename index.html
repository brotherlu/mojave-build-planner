How the numbers work

  • Starting skill: 2 + 2 × governing stat + half of Luck, rounded up. Tagged: +15.
  • Skill points per level: 10 + half of Intelligence, rounded down, +2 with Educated.
  • Hit points: 95 + 20 × Endurance + 5 × level. Action points: 65 + 3 × Agility.
  • Carry weight: 150 + 10 × Strength. Crit chance: Luck as a percent.

What it assumes

  • Skills are spent before the perk at the same level, as in the game, so this level's points count toward this level's perk.
  • Four Eyes assumes you wear glasses. Time of day, health and indoor traits are left out of the numbers.
  • Skill books and magazines are not counted. Leave headroom if you plan on reading.
